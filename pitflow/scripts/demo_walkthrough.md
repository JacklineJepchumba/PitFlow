# Demo walkthrough (about 3 minutes)

All data shown is simulated.

1. A household sends a USSD request and receives a price quote.
2. The dashboard shows three nearby jobs bundled into one route.
3. The emptier completes the trip and scans the PitStop QR at the treatment site. The household receives an SMS receipt.
4. The operator logs treatment readings; the rules engine marks the batch compliant.
5. A product QR passport is generated. Scanning it shows the chain from pit to product.
6. The funder dashboard shows tonnes treated, safe-disposal rate and product sold.
